# SPEC-ISSUE registry

Every `SPEC-ISSUE: LEAN-NNN` tag in `UsdSpec/**` has a row here. Status:
`candidate` (found by the formalization), `confirmed` (checked against the
specification and OpenUSD v26.11.1 source/runtime as applicable), `note`
(verified observation without an established specification defect),
`openusd-differs`, `rejected`, `drafted <NN>` (an
`issues/aousd/NN-*.md` draft exists; humans decide about filing).

Numbering: 0xx ListOp, 1xx Dict/LayerOffset, 2xx Path, 3xx Schema, 4xx Crate,
5xx Composition, 6xx Population, 7xx Value resolution, 8xx USDA grammar.
