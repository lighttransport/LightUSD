`candidate` (found by the formalization), `confirmed` (checked against OpenUSD
v26.11.1 source/runtime), `openusd-differs`, `rejected`, `drafted <NN>` (an
`issues/aousd/NN-*.md` draft exists; humans decide about filing).

5xx Composition, 6xx Population, 7xx Value resolution, 8xx USDA grammar.
